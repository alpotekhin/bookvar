=== PHYSICAL PAGE 76 OF 118 ===
Key challenge: Premise selection




   Automated theorem provers (ATPs) struggle with the large search space
   of possible proofs.

     • Premise selection: select a small subset of theorems and definitions
       that are likely to be useful for proving a given theorem.
         • Around 250, 000 premises in Lean’s Mathlib
         • Cuts down the prover’s search space
