# Community repositories

## Архитектура и обучение

- [nanochat](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat) — полный компактный pipeline.
- [LLMs-from-scratch](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch) — notebooks.
- [Annotated Transformer](https://github.com/harvardnlp/annotated-transformer) —
  original encoder-decoder.
- [LitGPT](https://github.com/Lightning-AI/litgpt) — читаемые model implementations.
- [Transformer Explainer](https://github.com/poloclub/transformer-explainer) —
  интерактивный visualizer.

## Post-training

- [TRL](https://github.com/huggingface/trl) — SFT, DPO, PPO, GRPO.
- [Open-R1](https://github.com/huggingface/open-r1) — reasoning/RLVR case study.
- [verl](https://github.com/verl-project/verl) — масштабируемый RL.
- [awesome-RLVR](https://github.com/opendilab/awesome-RLVR) — watchlist.

## Inference

- [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)
- [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)
- [SGLang](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang)
