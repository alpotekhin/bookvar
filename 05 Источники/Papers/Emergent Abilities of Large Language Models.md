# Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models

Jason Wei, Yi Tay, Rishi Bommasani et al., TMLR, 2022.
[Paper, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.07682v2), CC BY 4.0.

Sections 2–3 define the observed transition from near-chance
performance to above-chance performance and compare several model
families on few-shot tasks. Each point in [Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.07682v2#S3.F2)
is a separately trained model; its horizontal axis is training FLOPs.
The panel does not locate a universal parameter threshold.
Appendix A.1 compares task loss with discrete scores. Appendix A.2
reports three generative tasks where the apparent jump persists under
the available partial-credit metrics.

The figure is also shown at step 39 of
[Stanford CS336, Lecture 1](https://cs336.stanford.edu/lectures/?trace=lecture_01).
