# On the number of regions of piecewise linear neural networks

[Goujon, Etemadi and Unser, Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics 441](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/goujon2401.html),
article 115667, [DOI 10.1016/j.cam.2023.115667](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cam.2023.115667).

[§2.1–2.2](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/goujon2401.pdf#page=3)
defines continuous piecewise-linear maps and distinguishes projection
regions from convex linear regions. On each such region, the map agrees
with an affine function. The distinction matters for a whole network:
the set where a map agrees with one affine function need not be connected.
In [§3.3](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/goujon2401.pdf#page=9),
a Maxout output is the maximum over its learnable affine functions.

[Figure 6, page 7](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/goujon2401.pdf#page=7)
shows surfaces and input partitions for several activation constructions.
