# Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?

Rylan Schaeffer, Brando Miranda and Sanmi Koyejo,
[NeurIPS 2023](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/adc98a266f45005c403b8311ca7e8bd7-Abstract-Conference.html).
The textbook uses the unmodified multiplication panels in
[the versioned preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.15004v2#S3.F3), CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

Section 2 gives a simple model for exact-match probability and
partial-credit metrics. Section 3 keeps the arithmetic outputs fixed
while changing the scoring rule. It then increases the number of test
items in a separate experiment to measure rare exact matches.
These comparisons establish two explanations for apparent jumps
in the studied settings; they do not resolve every task or family.
