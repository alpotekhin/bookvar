## Source qualifications and editorial changes

The chapter does not turn a distribution change into a guaranteed quality drop,
a PSI threshold into a universal quality test, or a GPU-utilization percentage
into a measurement of useful work. It does not infer that an unchanged model
release excludes changes in live features, prompts, lookup tables or routing.
Diagnostic order follows the observed failure, rather than requiring every
incident to start by excluding infrastructure or to await full attribution
before mitigating ongoing harm.

The no-ai-slop skill and eval were read before substantial writing. The final
chapter was checked for generic setup, vague attribution, unsupported source
generalizations, definition-only fragments and repetitive emphasis. The
support-ticket example continues from the preceding chapter without presenting
its two validation conditions as complete release approval. Technical terms,
worked calculations, caveats and source wording are retained.

The exact Google metadata excerpt remains English, with only Markdown layout
changes. The checker normalizes whitespace and removes quote/list markers,
then compares the complete passage with the archived source. This preserves
the approved original wording rather than translating or stylistically
rewriting it.
