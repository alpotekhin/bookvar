## Reading and source checks

The original 556-line chapter was read in full before editing. The revised
chapter was read in full, then final wording and navigation edits were reviewed
as individual passages. Two read-only reviewers checked mathematical and
systems claims independently; the main editor checked primary passages and
reviewed all incorporated changes. A second mathematical review found no
remaining errors in the revised examples.

The source checks used Switch, ST-MoE, Expert Choice, DeepSeekMoE, DeepSeek-V2,
DeepSeek-V3, OLMoE, MegaBlocks, MiniCPM, LatentMoE, the Qwen1.5-MoE announcement
and configuration, and the versioned Megatron-Core 0.15 documentation. Exact
links are next to the relevant claims in the chapter.

Corrections and expansions:

- Parameter counts distinguish one layer from a sum over all MoE layers;
  input embedding lookup is not equated with activating its entire table.
- A worked top-2 example separates scores, selected indices, normalized
  weights, expert outputs and the residual connection.
- Top-1's task-loss gradient is derived. Unselected expert weights are
  distinguished from unselected logits in a full softmax denominator.
- Expert Choice's dependence on the routing group is explained with a
  displacement example; future-token competition is not causal generation.
- Fine-grained experts are compared at equal expert-parameter budgets;
  combinatorial route counts are not presented as independent learned networks.
- Expert counts and GPU placement have separate numerical examples. The
  Switch balance objective is not described as a distance to uniformity or
  as having a guaranteed lower bound of 1.
- Capacity is defined for a routing group; dropping an assignment is not
  deleting a token. Dropless kernels can still pad to block boundaries.
- Dispatch/combine are two logical forward phases, not a universal mandate
  for exactly two all-to-all API calls. Group-wide payload is distinguished
  from per-rank and physical network traffic.
- LatentMoE-eff and LatentMoE-acc have different active-expert and traffic
  budgets; router and shared experts remain in the original dimension.
- Z-loss regulates log-partition, not logit differences or expert balance.
  A common-shift example and an arbitrarily sharp zero-z-loss distribution
  demonstrate the distinction.
- ST-MoE's subset-update result is scoped to ST-MoE-L/SuperGLUE and its
  elevated training loss is distinguished from overfitting. The unsupported
  explanation of 1.4M versus 1.5M SFT examples is removed.
- Upcycling symmetry depends on weighted gradients, not merely identical
  inputs. MiniCPM's 13.6B excludes embeddings; 520B continued-pretraining
  tokens excludes the separate SFT. Qwen's recipe is not full-FFN cloning.
- DeepSeekMoE 16B did not use device-level balancing; the source slide's
  contrary implication is corrected explicitly in its caption. V2's limited
  outgoing fanout is separated from receive-side balancing.
- Serving includes expert-batch arithmetic and weight-memory bandwidth;
  no unconditional dense-versus-MoE or single-GPU-versus-cluster claim remains.

The main editor independently executed the numerical checks: 6,291,456
parameters per illustrative expert, 96 MiB total BF16 expert weights, capacity
1280, 128 MiB logical round-trip payload, balance objective 1.1, and log-partition
10.3132616875. The top-1 gradient agreed with finite differences within 2e-12
for a fixed-route example.

## Figures and provenance

The chapter now contains 22 images, not 22 newly added images. One composite
LatentMoE course screenshot was replaced by two source-native Figure 1 panels
from arXiv:2601.18089v1. Both PNG files retain their original bytes. Their
version-pinned URLs, SHA-256 hashes, authors and CC BY 4.0 license are recorded
in `05 Источники/asset-registry.yml`. The old course asset and frozen source
archive were not deleted or modified.

All figure boundaries were inspected at desktop and narrow widths. Source
plots, axes, legends and captions are not cropped by the page layout. This
does not imply every small label is readable at 390px: dense multi-panel
plots and full lecture slides require enlargement. A real browser click on
the DeepSeek slide opened its complete 1800x1014 original. The ST-MoE course
raster is only 438x368; its qualitative comparison remains readable, but a
higher-resolution, permission-verified replacement is still desirable.

This review does not convert legacy permission records into verified open
licenses. The two new LatentMoE originals have a verified named license;
older course/Hugging Face assets retain their existing provenance caveats.
