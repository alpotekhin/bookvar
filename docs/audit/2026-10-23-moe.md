## Explanation and style

The retained chapter already explained capacity, EP, shared/fine-grained
experts, upcycling and model differences in detail. It was expanded rather
than replaced by a short overview. Additions connect four sequential NumPy
blocks to worked examples:

- Four explicit input vectors, four complete ReLU FFNs, top-2 selection,
  grouping by expert and weighted return to the original token positions.
  Logits are supplied explicitly; the example does not pretend these arbitrary
  four probability rows came from a trained linear router for the chosen x.
- Fixed-route top-1 and normalized top-2 task gradients, including unselected
  logits versus unselected expert parameters. Normalizing top-1 to 1 removes
  this task-gradient path.
- Capacity keeps the first assignments in input order. The example shows the
  missing branches, a surviving zero-valued expert branch, and the residual;
  no silent renormalization or deletion of the token occurs.
- Balance and z-loss, their separate derivatives, a consistent balance value
  below 1, and stable log-sum-exp under a common logit shift.
- Biased selection with unbiased mixing scores; node-limited selection first
  scores groups, then chooses experts within allowed groups. The route-scale
  factor in the pinned implementation is named separately.
- A four-token/two-rank example distinguishes logical remote payload from
  physical network bytes. No network operation is executed by the example.

The no-ai-slop pass removed interpretive commentary, redundant recaps and
unnatural phrases without cutting the mechanisms or their caveats. The
old imperative recap became a concrete Switch/Mixtral/DeepSeek comparison.
Five wide display equations were split into readable lines. Three duplicate
formula screenshots were removed; the formulas remain live KaTeX. Two full
lecture-slide collages were removed: their repeated architecture picture was
already explained earlier, and their model facts remain in the prose with
primary links. All five old files and source archives were preserved.
