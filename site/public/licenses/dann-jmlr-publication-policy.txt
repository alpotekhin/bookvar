# JMLR publication policy checked 2026-10-28

Official source: https://jmlr.org/author-info.html#publication

The publication section states:

> When a paper is accepted, authors must sign an agreement granting a CC-by license to the public

The same paragraph states that copyright remains with the author.
The journal policy names CC-by but does not identify its version. The 2016
DANN PDF credits its authors. We record the publisher's CC-by policy,
not an invented CC BY 4.0 notice on the PDF or a separately inspected
author-signed agreement. The figure attribution identifies the authors,
article, original figure/page and extraction change.

The web browser source was read through the Publication section. A terminal
download of the policy stopped at 16037/22728 bytes; that incomplete HTML
is not used as the permission evidence or a complete source-page snapshot.
