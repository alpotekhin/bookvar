# PyTorch activation checkpointing figures

Two complete original figures from [Current and New Activation Checkpointing Techniques in PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/blog/activation-checkpointing-techniques/), by PyTorch contributors.

- `fg3.png`: eager forward/backward saved-activation dependencies.
- `fg12.png`: qualitative selective-checkpointing trade-off and memory budget. This is not a measured GPU-memory or throughput plot.

Images and BSD-3-Clause license are pinned to PyTorch website commit `563f92c6e87782196b81894c4e671428cd8d34af`. Published files are byte-identical to the original PNGs; English labels, arrows and complete image bounds are preserved. The archived HTML is a separately dated snapshot of the live article, not a claim that the current article text is pinned to that commit.
