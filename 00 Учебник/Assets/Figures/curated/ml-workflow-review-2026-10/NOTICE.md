# Original Google ML course figures

All six PNG images are from Google for Developers, Managing ML Projects,
ML pipelines, Figures 4-9. Source:
https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/managing-ml-projects/pipelines

Copyright Google. Content license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The images are complete, unchanged, byte-for-byte original copies. No crop,
redraw, translation, bitmap upscaling or other image editing was performed.
Original labels and language are retained. Adjacent chapter captions identify
each figure number, source title and license.

assets.json records source image locators, output/source hashes and archived
official-page license evidence. These records apply to these six original
course figures, not unrelated images or third-party photography.
