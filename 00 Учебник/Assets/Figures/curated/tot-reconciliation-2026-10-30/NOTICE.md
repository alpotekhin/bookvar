# Tree of Thoughts figures

Shunyu Yao, Dian Yu, Jeffrey Zhao, Izhak Shafran, Thomas L. Griffiths,
Yuan Cao and Karthik Narasimhan, *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem
Solving with Large Language Models*, NeurIPS 2023, arXiv v2.

[Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601v2),
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Figures 4, 5 and 6 retain all original panels, labels and English captions.
Only surrounding page material was cropped; no content was redrawn,
translated or retouched. The archived PDF, rendered pages, crop rectangles
and hashes are recorded in assets.json and the source packet. The paper's
claims and screenshots do not constitute a local model benchmark.
