# Original consistency-training illustrations

The two PNGs retain the complete original figures and captions from the
attributed ACL papers, rendered at 400 dpi. The selected PDF bounds only
remove surrounding article text. No labels or panels have been redrawn.
The ACL copyright policy archived with the sources licenses post-2016
ACL publications under CC BY 4.0. This does not license unrelated arXiv papers.

The VAT GIF is Takeru Miyato's original 14-frame file, copied byte-for-byte
from the pinned MIT repository. Its native 320×180 resolution is retained.
The copyright and MIT permission notice are preserved in the public license copy.

Exact source links, coordinates, dimensions, hashes and chapter destinations
are recorded in assets.json and the global asset registry.
