# Original BERTScore illustration

Tianyi Zhang, Varsha Kishore, Felix Wu, Kilian Q. Weinberger and Yoav Artzi,
*BERTScore: Evaluating Text Generation with BERT*.

The complete original `bert_score.png` from the authors' repository is copied
byte-for-byte. It retains every stage, token label, matrix entry, weight and
formula. No crop, redraw, translation, retouching or recompression.
