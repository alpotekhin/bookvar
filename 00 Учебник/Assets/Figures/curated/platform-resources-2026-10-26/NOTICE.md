# Original resource-management and tenancy figures

The two complete SVGs are from Kubernetes SIGs Kueue, commit
`bca571a3dae56ebca83109ae6769bade1c73dcfa`, Copyright 2022 The Kubernetes
Authors, Apache-2.0. They show Workload/LocalQueue/ClusterQueue relationships
and cohort/resource-flavor relationships. Both remain byte-for-byte copies;
the source SVG viewBoxes and labels are unchanged. The full license is
retained in the source archive and `site/public/licenses/kueue-bca571-LICENSE.txt`.

The complete multi-tenancy PNG is from The Kubernetes Authors,
`kubernetes/website` commit `ed70e311737a341574ed5758b7af24e3bba232cb`,
CC BY 4.0. It shows coexisting team/customer tenancy models, not automatically
enforced security boundaries. Its full source license is retained in the
archive and `site/public/licenses/kubernetes-ed70e3-LICENSE.txt`.

No figure is cropped, redrawn, translated or recompressed. Each adjacent
caption links directly to its pinned source page. Full hashes, authors,
licenses, source snapshots and modification statements are in assets.json
and the global registry. The independent Harvard-derived narrative/examples
use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; that does not replace these originals' licenses.
