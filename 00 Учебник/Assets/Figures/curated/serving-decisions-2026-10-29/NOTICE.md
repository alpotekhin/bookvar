# Original course figure

`retraining-policies.png` is an unchanged copy of Figure 8, *The Rotting Asset
Curve*, from Vijay Janapa Reddi and Harvard ML Systems contributors,
*ML Operations*. The original illustrates scheduled and triggered updates
under an assumed degradation process; it is not an empirical model-lifetime
benchmark. The chapter states its instantaneous-restoration assumptions.
