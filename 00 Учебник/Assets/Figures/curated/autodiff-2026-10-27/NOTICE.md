# Automatic differentiation: original graph and traces

Atılım Güneş Baydin, Barak A. Pearlmutter, Alexey Andreyevich Radul and
Jeffrey Mark Siskind. *Automatic Differentiation in Machine Learning: a Survey*.
JMLR 18 (153), 2018, pp. 1–43.
[Publisher record](https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/17-468.html).

Figure 4 (p. 9), Table 2 (p. 10) and Table 3 (p. 13) retain the complete
original objects and complete English captions. They were rendered from
the vector PDF at 400 dpi, excluding unrelated page text and margins only.
No labels, numbers, arrows or colors were changed. No translated caption
was substituted into the images.
