# Original synthetic-data figures

The three PNGs are unchanged originals by Suriya Gunasekar et al., Marah Abdin et al., and NVIDIA, respectively. They are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Article, figure and direct-image URLs, retained license evidence, exact bytes and hashes are listed in assets.json. No figure was cropped, recolored, relabeled, redrawn or enlarged. Captions and explanations in the chapter are editorial additions. PNG transparency is retained; the site's existing white figure background preserves the original dark labels.
