# ReAct original figures

Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan and Yuan Cao. ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models, ICLR 2023, arXiv 2210.03629v3. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629v3. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Figures 2 and 3 are complete crops from the 300-dpi PDF page renders, including both panels, axes, legend and the full English captions. Only surrounding paper text was excluded. No translation, redraw or retouching. Exact source hashes, page coordinates and license evidence are in assets.json. The page rendering/crop script is retained in the source packet.
