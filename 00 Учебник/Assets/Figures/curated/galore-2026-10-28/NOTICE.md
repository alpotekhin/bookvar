# GaLoRE original illustrations

Jiawei Zhao, Zhenyu Zhang, Beidi Chen, Zhangyang Wang, Anima Anandkumar
and Yuandong Tian, GaLore: Memory-Efficient LLM Training by Gradient Low-Rank
Projection (2024).

Both complete PNG files are unchanged copies from the authors' repository,
commit `2cc66f88cce189e505affbb91042a8e77f5bf4e9`, `imgs/`.
The repository distributes them under Apache License 2.0; no separate image
license or NOTICE was found in the pinned recursive tree. The full license
is in the source archive and `/licenses/galore-2cc66f88-Apache-2.0.txt`.

No crop, redraw, translation, relabeling or recompression was performed.
The accompanying Bookvar explanation distinguishes a low-rank step from
a full-rank accumulated change, and illustrative pseudocode from executed
optimizer code. Hashes, pinned URLs and dimensions are in `assets.json`.

Figure 1, `memory_breakdown.svg`, is a separate unchanged copy from
`https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03507v2/memory_breakdown.svg`.
The paper is licensed CC BY 4.0, as declared by its arXiv v2 abstract page;
that declaration is archived as `arxiv-abs-v2.html`. This license is not
the repository's Apache 2.0 license. The figure and complete English
caption/footnote are attributed beside the image. Estimates for a 256-token
single-device batch are not relabeled as measured Bookvar GPU results.
