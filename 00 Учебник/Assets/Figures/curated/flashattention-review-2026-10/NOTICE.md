## Files and transformations

- `fa3-pingpong-original.png`: 3285 × 671, unchanged archive member
  `figs/pingpong_pipelining.png` from <https://arxiv.org/src/2407.08608v1>.
  This is the original-resolution source, not the 2048-pixel HTML rendition.
- `fa3-gemm-softmax-original.png`: 652 × 140, unchanged download from
  <https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08608v1/figs/2_stage_pipelining.png>.
  Its transparency is preserved. The website supplies a white image background.
  The source PDF and author blog contain the same low-resolution diagram;
  enlarging it does not create additional detail.
