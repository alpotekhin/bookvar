# Original two-layer network

Andrej Karpathy, Stanford CS231n, Neural Networks Part 1, Layer-wise organization.
The complete original neural_net.jpeg is copied byte-for-byte from commit
8ec57ccfc32379ae4b37c45de949af359673f92f. Its three input units, four hidden
units, two output units, arrows and English labels are unchanged.

The diagram shows the architecture, not a runtime trace or the dimensions of
the 784–256–10 worked model. The chapter states this distinction explicitly.
The 511×350 JPEG is kept at its source resolution, without artificial upscaling
or recompression. UI sizing does not add source detail.
