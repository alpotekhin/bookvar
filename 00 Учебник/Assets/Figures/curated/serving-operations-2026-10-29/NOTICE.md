# Original Harvard course figures

Two byte-identical SVGs copied from the existing source import at commit
`2bd97c509923dc8d7cb0b3e2f489a7282fee5fbd`. Authors: Vijay Janapa Reddi and
Harvard ML Systems contributors. License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Pinned license
was checked again on 2026-10-29 and matches the local evidence file.

`assets.json` records source URLs, local source paths, hashes, captions and
license evidence. The power-boundary figure is the course's own complete
diagram following Tschand et al.; it is not a newly drawn Bookvar figure.
Original texts, shapes, colours and geometry were not changed. PNG previews
were used only for inspection and are not publication assets.

The existing Harvard `model_serving_blast_radius.svg` was reviewed and
rejected: its unlabelled square, circles and arrows do not explain fan-out
timing or hedge cancellation. The original ML Test Score PDF carries an
IEEE redistribution notice, so no paper figure or full paper text was
copied under the course's license.
