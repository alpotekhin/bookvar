# Data augmentation: original figures

The files below retain the authors' original image bytes. They were not
redrawn, cropped, translated, upscaled or recompressed. `assets.json` binds
each file to its archived source, page, license, hashes and chapter usage.

## CutMix comparison

`cutmix-comparison.png`, 649 × 447, is `img1.PNG` from
[NAVER's CutMix repository](https://github.com/clovaai/CutMix-PyTorch/blob/2d8eb68faff7fe4962776ad51d175c3b01a25734/README.md).
Authors: Sangdoo Yun, Dongyoon Han, Seong Joon Oh, Sanghyuk Chun, Junsuk Choe
and Youngjoon Yoo. Copyright 2019-present NAVER Corp., MIT; the complete
original notice is in `site/public/licenses/cutmix-2d8eb68-MIT.txt`.

This is the repository overview, not the paper's CAM Figure 1. Its lower
rows retain historical classification, localization and detection results
with different tasks and metrics. They are not local measurements. The
license of this released figure is not a blanket license for the underlying
ImageNet dataset or every other photograph on the web.

## D2L random crops

`d2l-random-crops.svg` is the complete eight-panel output associated with
the PyTorch RandomResizedCrop example in
[Dive into Deep Learning, Image Augmentation](https://d2l.ai/chapter_computer-vision/image-augmentation.html#flipping-and-cropping).
Authors: Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li and Alexander J. Smola.
CC BY-SA 4.0; the complete notice is in
`site/public/licenses/d2l-23d7a5a-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`.

The published output and its HTML snapshot are pinned independently of
the book's source-code commit. The gallery was downloaded, not regenerated
by executing our examples. Its eight raster panels remain embedded in
the original self-contained SVG.
