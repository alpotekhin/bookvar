# Original speculative-decoding figures

EAGLE-2 Figure 7 is taken from the published EMNLP 2024 PDF under the ACL Anthology's CC BY 4.0 terms. The EAGLE feature/tree diagram is copied unchanged from the official SafeAILab/EAGLE repository at commit cb7e0841fe0c206c6ed74a197ad5e2a1f13f5a2b under Apache-2.0.

EAGLE Figure 6 (shifted token/feature input) is extracted from the ICML 2024 publication, PMLR 235:28935-28948, under CC BY 4.0. The original publication is https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/li24bt.html; PMLR's publication agreement grants this license in clause 2 and requires the PMLR citation/link in clause 3.

All authors, direct source URLs, licenses, hashes, archive members, source PDFs, page/render dimensions, and crop coordinates are recorded in assets.json. Raster copies are unchanged. Both standalone PDF figures are full-page renders. The complete EAGLE Figure 6 and EAGLE-2 Figure 7 artworks are cropped from surrounding paper text; no diagram elements were removed, redrawn or translated.

No new EAGLE-3 artwork is reproduced: its original Figures 5 and 6 are linked in the chapter. A permissive license for those figures was not established in this review; the arXiv non-exclusive distribution license is not labeled CC BY.
