# Original Ulysses and Ring figures

The two source objects are preserved completely and byte-for-byte, with their English labels and full image bounds. No source diagram has been redrawn.

## Ulysses

Figure 2 of *DeepSpeed Ulysses: System Optimizations for Enabling Training of Extreme Long Sequence Transformer Models*, by Sam Ade Jacobs et al., arXiv:2309.14509v2. The version-specific arXiv abstract declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Both the article and its license declaration are archived; exact URLs and hashes are in assets.json. The special case P = head count = 4 and the meaning of the mathematical attention matrix are explained alongside the complete figure.

## Ring animation

Original ring-attention.gif from *The Ultra-Scale Playbook*, by Nouamane Tazi et al., Hugging Face. The image, source article HTML and license declaration are pinned to commit c93890dd4b22356ee18ed9b863e6f2d0909729bc. The README declares Apache-2.0; its complete text is distributed at [/licenses/ultrascale-Apache-2.0.txt](/licenses/ultrascale-Apache-2.0.txt). All frames and their timing are retained. This is a qualitative animation, not a measured performance timeline.

The Ring Attention paper has its own arXiv license; this notice does not assign the Playbook's Apache license to artwork in that paper. The imported GIF is the Playbook source object, not a paper figure.
