# Binomial-tree animation

Flogr, *Binomial tree*, 2 February 2018.
[Original file and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Binomial_tree.gif&oldid=824382071).
Licensed by its author under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The complete 901 × 512 GIF is copied unchanged. All four frames and their
1500 ms durations are retained. Its eight input values are 2, 3, 5, 1, 7,
6, 8 and 4; the final sum is 36. This is an abstract dependency diagram,
not a measured GPU timeline or a fixed mapping onto CUDA lanes.

Source bytes, description-page snapshot and checksums are recorded in
`assets.json`. The chapter's CUDA helper is separate software from NVIDIA
CUDA Samples, revision `5443602`, under its BSD-3-Clause notice.
The historical Harris PDF was read as a technical source; no permission
to redistribute its slide images is asserted or borrowed from CUDA Samples.
