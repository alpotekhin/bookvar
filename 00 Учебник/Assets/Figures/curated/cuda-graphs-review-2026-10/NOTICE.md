# CUDA Graphs source figures

Vinh Nguyen, Michael Carilli, Sukru Burc Eryilmaz, Vartika Singh,
Michelle Lin, Natalia Gimelshein, Alban Desmaison and Edward Yang,
*Accelerating PyTorch with CUDA Graphs*, October 26, 2021:
https://pytorch.org/blog/accelerating-pytorch-with-cuda-graphs/

The three PNGs are unchanged copies of Figures 1, 3 and 4. Their source files
are retained in `pytorch/pytorch.github.io` at commit
`d1050dad1fe8ff2b28e1672d6b9f3eaa33e91320`:

- `assets/images/cuda-image-2.png` → `launch-vs-replay.png`
- `assets/images/cuda-image-4.png` → `mask-rcnn-before.png`
- `assets/images/cuda-image-5.png` → `mask-rcnn-after.png`

No crop, resizing, redraw, relabeling or translation has been applied.
The full website-repository BSD 3-Clause copyright notice and disclaimer
are retained in `LICENSE-website-BSD-3-Clause.txt`. The figures are historical
source illustrations, not new traces measured by Bookvar. Screenshot scales
and layouts differ; compare their labelled durations rather than their widths.
