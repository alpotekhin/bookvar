# PCA, t-SNE and UMAP source figures

All seven images are complete original PNGs, with no crop, relabeling,
translation, redrawing or pixel resampling. Browser fit-to-container
display does not change their stored pixels.

output at /cells/18/outputs/0/data/image~1png. The notebook was not executed
